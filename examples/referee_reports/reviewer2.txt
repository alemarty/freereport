Report on "A Short Study of Tutoring and Exam Scores"

I enjoyed this paper, but have concerns.

(a) The effect size is reported as 0.43 in the abstract, while the text says 0.34. These must be reconciled.

(b) The paper never states that assignment was random, so the estimate may be confounded.

(c) The p-value of 0.000 in Table 2 should be reported as p < 0.001.
